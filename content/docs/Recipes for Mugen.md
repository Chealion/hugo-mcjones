# Recipes for Mugen
[Hario Mugen](https://www.hario-canada.ca/products/hario-v60-mugen-dripper-plastic)

Hario’s V60 like brewer with much, much less bypass options. I find it similar to a Chemex without the Chemex filter.
## Hario
20g coffee  
240g water

Single pour. Let draw down ~ 2 minutes

My notes:  
Find it on the thin side but solid.

## Reddit Recipes
https://new.reddit.com/r/pourover/comments/17dzs3u/mugen_how_is_it_so_good_is_the_v60_a_lie/

17g coffee  
270g water  
One pour

Takes 2:15-2:30

My notes:  
Works nicer than Hario’s if you grind finer.
