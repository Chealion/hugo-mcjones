# Roasted Veg Timings
## Broccoli
400F / 205C
35 min

Toss with oil, salt, pepper

## Asparagus
400F / 205C
12-15 min

Toss with oil, salt, pepper.
If adding parmesan, add after 10 min

## Brussel Sprouts
400F / 205C
45 min

## Carrots
400F / 205C
45 min

## Cauliflower
450F / 230C
20 min

Toss with oil, salt, pepper
Add lemon juice just before servicing

## Parsnips
425F / 220C
30 min

Toss with olive oil, salt, pepper
